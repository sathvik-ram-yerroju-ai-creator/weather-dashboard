Under the Hood

Technology & Inference Architecture

A four-stage pipeline built on custom tensor kernels and distributed GPU clusters.

Step 01

Multimodal Tokenizer

Unified byte-pair and patch encoding for text, source code, tables, and visual inputs.

Step 02

Sparse Mixture-of-Experts

Activates specialized neural sub-networks per token to maximize reasoning depth per FLOP.

Step 03

Speculative Decoding

Draft-and-verify parallel token generation delivering 2.8x faster streaming throughput.

Step 04

Safety & Verification Layer

Post-inference policy checks and citation grounding prior to client delivery.
